# Declared-values audit, 2026-09-07

Author instruction: *"can you also do another audit for made up #s while you're at it"*, after the
two flow-archetype operating points turned out to be declared conventions that measured films on
disk could have replaced (see `ARCHETYPE_REANCHOR_20260907.md`).

Generated by `data/declared_values_audit.py` from `data/parameters_provenance.csv` and
`results/assumption_ledger.json`. The registry holds **292 rows, 128 of them assumption-class**.
Every assumption row is listed; the *action* column is the audit's judgement.

## The rule applied

A state-C row is acceptable when (i) it is an operator choice or modelling convention that no
measurement could replace, or (ii) no page-anchored measurement of that quantity exists in the tree,
and in either case it carries a sensitivity. It is a **defect** when a page-anchored measurement
of the same quantity is on disk and was not adopted, which is what the flow films were. The search
for replacements was made against the PDFs in `papers for model/`, `Model Papers for Params/` and
`papers for model/concentrations/SIs/` (including the `final upload/zotero uploads` subtree).

## Findings

- **Two rows were defects and are retired** (the flow-archetype operating points). Both were
  disclosed as assumptions with swept bands; the defect was that measured films existed on disk.
- **No other assumption row has a page-anchored measurement of its own quantity in the tree.** The
  thermal-model reactors of Fig. 5 (category 9) are the largest declared block: five archetype
  geometries, four design currents, four internal film coefficients and three housing factors, all
  declared and all swept in Table S7i. They are flagged below as DECLARED-KEEP because no cell
  geometry or design current is measured anywhere in the tree; they should be read as the model's
  own definitions of its reactors, which the SI states.
- **The 34 electrolyte conductivities with no source** are the block the kappa dossier already
  audited; nothing new was found, and G-KAPPA/G-MSKAPPA bind every one the documents quote.
- **The stirred mediated band edges were stale** (50-200 um, the retired declared band, while the
  NP rows used 193-207 um); corrected in this pass (`make_bounds_runs.py`).

## Category rules

- **1.** declared modelling convention (temperature): no measurement replaces a chosen operating temperature.
- **2.** mixed-solvent mu/rho with no tabulated 25 C value; each carries a citable bound (G-MIXBOUND) or says no bound exists.
- **3.** declared modelling choices (theory level, viscosity used, hydrodynamic radius, an unused Le Bas increment).
- **4.** solver-species diffusivities that are declared transfers or class defaults for spectator ions; G-DSENS proves the spectator set moves nothing (exactly zero), and the mediator/substrate base-case constants are bounded by G-ECPANEL.
- **5.** electron counts from the balanced half-reactions and a trace-initialisation rule; not measurable quantities.
- **6.** electrolyte conductivities with no measured value for that exact composition; the sourcing dossier (docs/KAPPA_SOURCING_DOSSIER.md) records what was retrieved, and every row carries a band.
- **7.** reactor operating points (rotation speeds, cylinder diameter, free-convection height and driving force) and the Leveque coefficient; operator choices that no measurement replaces, each with a swept band.
- **8.** voltage-stack floor and exchange current for the thermal balance; declared, and E0 cancels out of the dissipation entirely.
- **9.** thermal-model geometries, design currents and film coefficients for the five Fig. 5 reactors; DECLARED archetypes (no measured cell geometry is on disk for any of them), each swept in Table S7i.
- **10.** order-of-magnitude homogeneous rate constants with literature anchors per row (Table S6); G-KSENS states what a tenfold revision costs.
- **11.** numerical settings, verified against analytic limits (S5.6); not physical quantities.

## What this audit cannot do

not in the tree. A row marked DECLARED-KEEP is a row for which nothing on disk displaces the
declaration, not a row whose declaration is beyond question.

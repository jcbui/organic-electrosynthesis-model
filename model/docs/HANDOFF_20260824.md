## One decision is waiting for you

**The carrier concentration on the two rows where a LIMITED BASE creates an anionic carrier.**

| row | declares | its base supplies | deficit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kolbe homocoupling of 10-undecenoate | 1.00 M carboxylate | 0.20 M Me₄N⁺ (30 mmol Me₄NOH + 10 mmol Me₄NBF₄ / 200 mL) | **5.0×** |
| Non-Kolbe decarboxylative alpha-methoxylation | 0.10 M carboxylate | 7.5 mM Et₃N | **13.3×** |

Neither is electroneutral as written; the solver manufactures the shortfall. Three readings are
defensible and differ by up to an order of magnitude:

1. **carrier = the carboxylate actually present** (0.15 M / 7.5 mM) — electroneutral, self-consistent
2. **carrier = the neutral acid, z = 0** — no migration, full inventory transported
3. **a CE system** — fast acid ⇌ carboxylate pre-equilibrium with the acid as a mobile reservoir,
   so the transported quantity is the total even though the discharged species is dilute. Same
   structure as the mediated EC′ rows; the model does not express it today.

`julia/carboxylate_decision.jl` solves all three, at all six architectures, so you can choose on
numbers. **These two rows' ceilings should not be quoted until this is settled.**

This is bounded: all nine other charged-carrier rows balance exactly. It is one chemical situation,
not a scattered problem.
